Delete Your Account - DistroNote

To delete your DistroNote account and remove all your personal data from our system, please follow the steps below:

  1. Open the DistroNote app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Ensure your subscription is already inactive (not active or expired).
  4. Navigate to the Subscription / Account page.
  5. Tap the Delete Account button.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from our system. This action cannot be undone.

If you need help or cannot access your account, you can request deletion assistance via email:

Email: distronote@gmail.com

We will verify your request and process the deletion manually if needed.
